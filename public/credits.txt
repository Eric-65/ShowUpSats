Photography
-----------
All 13 photos in /public/img were supplied by the project owner for this hackathon build.
Each one was processed with scripts/process-images.ts (greyscale, crushed blacks, high contrast,
vignette, film grain, WebP) to give the site one consistent dark editorial look.
The originals are not committed.

Before any public launch, confirm the licence of each source photo (e.g. Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay
licence), credit the photographers here, and make sure none shows a public figure.
